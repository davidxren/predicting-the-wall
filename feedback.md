Very good proposal. The topic is clearly scoped and handled. The one thing I'd keep in mind is year-to-year variation in race conditions. Race-day weather in particular can shift the overall slowdown rate a lot between years, and the 2021 race was a smaller October field because of COVID. That means your 2024 test year could have a very different base rate than training, which affects both average precision and calibration. Report the base rate per year up front, and consider whether adding race-day temperature as a feature (or at least discussing it) would help explain differences. Also remember that Boston's course profile (net downhill early, Newton hills late) makes positive splits the norm, so check what fraction of runners clear the 10% threshold. If it's very high or very low, the 5% or 15% cutoffs may end up being more informative.
